Tip 1: Tune your strings so Sa matches your comfortable tonic — many players in Malaysia tune Sa to C or D on violin.

Tip 2: Slide (gamaka) between swaras on one string instead of lifting the finger — it gives the Carnatic feel.

Tip 3: Practise slowly with a tanpura drone app; keep the four fingers on one string before crossing strings.
